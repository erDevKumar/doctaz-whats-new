01A redesigned Community feed

Posts are easier to read and their photos, videos and documents are easier to browse, right in the feed.

Each post shows who wrote it, their role, when, and who it's for, with the text and media beneath.

02Change your password

Anyone can now change their password from inside the app.

Where to find it

Menu → Manage My Account → Change Password.

Android

iPhone

Simple form

Enter the current password and the new one twice. The app checks they match before sending.

Smaller fixes you may notice.

Also improved

  • MedTalk chats show people's names reliably on Android.
  • Message notifications arrive more reliably.

What's new in Doctaz

Android 2.5.3 → 2.5.5 · iPhone 2.5.3 → 2.5.5

Everything below is new since the versions you last received. Each feature shows the Android app and the iPhone app side by side, captured from demo accounts talking to each other (patients, nurses and doctors).

  • Messages, rebuilt around your conversations
  • See who you are talking to in a discussion
  • “Solo” is now called “Virtual Care”
  • A tidier home screen
  • Sharing into Doctaz from another app
  • Fully translated into French, Spanish and Arabic
  • Stability

01Messages, rebuilt around your conversations

MedTalk now opens straight onto your conversations instead of a set of tabs. Everything is one tap away: search at the top, filters underneath, and your chats below.

Your chats, with filters

Open MedTalk and your conversations are already there. Filter to Unread, Providers or Patients, and each row shows the person's role and what the last message was — a photo, a document or a link.

iPhone

Search people and chats together

Type a name and results split into your existing chats and everyone else, with the matched part of the name highlighted. Filter by Doctors, Nurses or Patients.

Android

iPhone

Doctaz Support, always one tap away

The headphones button at the top right of MedTalk opens a conversation with Doctaz Support from anywhere in your chats.

Android

iPhone

A clearer conversation

Each chat now shows who you are talking to — their photo, name and role — at the top. The message bar has a camera button for sending a photo straight away.

Android

iPhone

Also improved

  • The video-call button in a chat was white on a white background on Android, so it looked missing even though it worked. It is now clearly visible.
  • Doctors and nurses can now find and message each other, not only patients.
  • On iPhone, losing signal shows a notice that messages will send once you reconnect.

02See who you are talking to in a discussion

Community discussions used to show only a role, like “Doctor” or “Member”. Every discussion now leads with the person — their photo, their name and their role — and you can tap through to their profile.

Who started each discussion

The list shows the author's photo and name on every card, with the number of replies and views, and a button to start your own that stays put as you scroll.

iPhone

Author shown on the discussion itself

Discussions with a chosen group

You can start a discussion that only chosen people may reply to. The card shows it is members-only and how many have joined.

iPhone

See everyone in the discussion

Tap the members row to see everyone, each with their photo and role. As the person who started it, you also see who has accepted, who is still invited and who declined.

iPhone

Also improved

  • Invitations now say who invited you, with their photo and name, instead of only naming the discussion.
  • Reply and view counts on a discussion card update as soon as you come back from reading it, instead of looking stale.
  • Wording like “1 views” and “1 discussions” now reads correctly.
  • On Android, the bottom bar no longer sits on top of the keyboard and hides what you are typing.

03“Solo” is now called “Virtual Care”

The space you work in on your own — outside any hospital — is now called Virtual Care, which says what it is. Hospital environments are unchanged.

Pick where you are working

After signing in, choose Virtual Care or a hospital you belong to. The new name appears here, in the app's title bar and anywhere access is requested.

Android

iPhone

Also improved

  • Translated along with everything else, so it reads correctly in French, Spanish and Arabic.

04A tidier home screen

The shortcuts at the top of the home screen — MedCue, MedTalk, MedMall and notifications — are now in the Doctaz brand colour and grouped neatly to the right, instead of grey and loosely spaced.

Shortcuts in the brand colour

The same change on both phones, so the two apps look like one product.

Android

iPhone

05Sharing into Doctaz from another app

Sending a photo, document or link from another app into a Doctaz chat has been rebuilt on both phones: search and pick several people, see exactly what you are sending, and add a caption before it goes.

Also improved

  • The people list no longer stops loading more results when you filter by role.
  • On Android, sharing while signed out now keeps what you were sending and carries on after you sign in.
  • Files that cannot be sent now say so clearly, instead of failing quietly.
  • On iPhone, if your session has expired the share sheet now tells you to sign in rather than showing an error.

06Fully translated into French, Spanish and Arabic

Large parts of the apps were still appearing in English for anyone using them in another language. Registration, your profile, the wallet, messages and the community are now fully translated on both phones.

Also improved

  • Around 180 pieces of text on Android and over 1,100 on iPhone were translated.
  • The search box text was shortened so it is no longer cut off.

07Stability

Two crashes that interrupted everyday use are fixed.

Also improved

  • Closing the “Coming Soon” notice no longer closes the app with it.
  • The low-balance warning during a video call no longer closes the app.
  • On Android, cropped videos in the community feed no longer spill over the post title and the buttons underneath.
  • On iPhone, the menu button on your profile no longer looks like a back arrow.
Screens captured on a Nothing Phone (Android) and an iPhone 17 simulator, both running version 2.5.5, using demo accounts talking to each other. Everything shown is demo content.
